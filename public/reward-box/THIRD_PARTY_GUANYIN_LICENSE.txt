QinTianJian (钦天监) — full traditional Guanyin lots dataset
Copyright © 2026 肆月Aperture
Source repository: https://github.com/zouzuo1994321/QinTianJian
Source file: qintianjian/data/sample_signs.py

Only the Guanyin 100 lots corpus was extracted for the morning-fortune feature in this application.
The source repository has two entries each for numbers 6 and 26: first occurrence retained so the module contains 100 unique numbers.
The traditional Chinese text, imagery and explanations are culturally situated folk material. This integration is for cultural interest, reflection and entertainment, not a reliable prediction, decision system or professional advice.
